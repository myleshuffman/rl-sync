## Filter Parameter Syntax

- **Built-in status**: `status.eq=<value>`
- **Custom field (exact match)**: `project.field.<fieldId>.value=<value>`
- **Custom field (contains)**: `project.field.<fieldId>.contains=<text>`
- **Team name**: `team.cn=<name>`
- **Pagination**: `limit.eq=<n>`
